Escape Game: Wa no Yado

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026 · Last updated: October 19, 2026 · Operator: ZU YIBO (individual developer)

This page explains how the iOS and Android (Google Play) apps "Escape Game: Wa no Yado (脱出ゲーム 和の宿)" (the "App") handles information.

Summary: The App has no accounts and no servers, and stores game progress only on your device. It shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect advertising-related information.

1. Information collected by the developer

The developer does not collect personal information such as your name or email address. There are no accounts or logins and no developer servers. Game progress is stored only on your device and is never sent elsewhere.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (the advertising ID: on iOS, the IDFA, only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt; on Android, the Google advertising ID)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance data

See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can turn off personalised ads in iOS under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Settings > Google (on some devices, Privacy) > Ads.

3. In-app purchase

The App offers one optional in-app purchase, "Remove ads". Payment is processed by Apple (iOS) or Google (Android); the developer receives no payment details such as card numbers.

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.
