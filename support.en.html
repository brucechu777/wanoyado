How to play

  • Look around the room and tap anything that looks interesting to examine it.
  • Items you find go into your inventory. Select an item, then tap where you think it can be used.
  • Combine clues, solve the puzzles, and escape the room.

Hints

If you get stuck, use the in-game hint feature. It also helps to check for spots you have not examined yet and to look at your inventory again.

Restore purchase

If you bought "Remove ads" and ads appear after switching devices or reinstalling, tap "Restore Purchase" in the game. You must be signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.
